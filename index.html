Common questions

How do I import an Anki deck?

Tap the ••• menu on the deck list and choose Import deck, then pick a .apkg file. You can also open a .apkg from Files, Mail, or any other app and choose Anamn. Cards, media, and your review history all come across. Re-importing an updated version of a deck you already have will update the existing notes rather than duplicating them.

Can I get my decks back out?

Always. Open a deck and tap the share button, or use Export deck in the ••• menu, to save a standard .apkg file that Anki and other apps can read. Exporting is never restricted — it works during the trial, after the trial ends, and whether or not you have bought anything. Your collection is yours.

How does the free trial work?

You get seven days with everything unlocked. After that, a one-time purchase unlocks studying, creating and editing cards, and importing decks. There is no subscription and no account.

If you decide not to buy, nothing is deleted or locked away: you can still browse all of your cards and export every deck.

I already paid, but a new phone is asking me to buy again

Open the ••• menu, choose Help, and tap Restore purchases. Make sure the device is signed in to the same Apple Account you bought with — purchases are tied to the Apple Account, not the device.

My decks are not syncing between devices

Check the ••• menu → iCloud Sync. That screen shows whether sync is switched on, whether the device is signed in to iCloud, and when it last synced successfully.

The most common cause is full iCloud storage — if your iCloud is out of space, Apple refuses new data and syncing stops. The sync screen will tell you when this is happening. Your decks stay safe on the device regardless.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything is stored on your device, and no internet connection is needed to study. iCloud sync, if you turn it on, is the only part that uses the network.
