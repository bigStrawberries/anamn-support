Privacy Policy

Anamn · Last updated 20 August 2026

Anamn does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. There are no accounts, no servers of ours, no analytics, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

What stays on your device

Your decks, cards, review history, imported media, and settings are stored locally on your iPhone. We have no ability to see any of it.

Photos and microphone

You can attach an image or record audio for a card. Images you pick and audio you record are saved into the app's own storage on your device and are used only to display that card. They are not uploaded anywhere. The app requests access to your microphone only at the moment you choose to record, and reads a photo only when you pick one.

iCloud sync (optional, off by default)

If you switch on iCloud Sync, your decks are mirrored to your own private iCloud database so they can move between your devices and survive a lost phone. This uses Apple's iCloud infrastructure and your personal Apple Account. The developer has no access to that data and no way to read it. It is governed by Apple's privacy policy. Turning the setting off stops further syncing.

Purchases

The one-time purchase that unlocks the app is handled entirely by Apple's App Store. We never see your name, payment details, or Apple Account. The app only ever asks Apple a yes-or-no question: has this purchase been made? The seven-day trial is tracked on your device alone.

Notifications

Study reminders, if you enable them, are scheduled locally on your device. Nothing is sent through any server, and no push tokens are collected.

If you email us

Reporting an issue from inside the app opens your own mail app with a message you write and can edit before sending. Nothing is gathered from your device automatically. If you do email, we will have your email address and whatever you chose to write, used only to reply to you and fix the problem. We do not add you to any mailing list.

Children

Anamn is safe for all ages. Because it collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
